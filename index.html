    Hola, I’m Steph

    Passionate about the craft and its power to break language, social, and cultural barriers, I’m a designer transforming complexity into impossible-to-ignore visuals. I’ve spent the last eight years building identities, editorial systems and campaigns for teams in mobility, fintech and climate.

    Currently, I lead brand design at Abatable, where I work at the intersection of design, climate, and tech. I build scalable design systems that empower teams to create on-brand assets efficiently, and I’m always looking for innovative ways to make an abstract market more accessible.

    Made in Colombia and based in London, I care about design that does real work in the world. I have a master’s in Design for Social Innovation and Sustainable Futures (UAL), I’m a soon-to-be-published researcher, and I am part of the Marketing team at Empower LATAM UK.

    What this prototype shows

    • Two views (top-left toggles on Home and Work): carousel and gallery. Choice is remembered.
    • Carousel: centre card grows; drag, scroll, arrow keys or dots to move. Click the centre card to open it.
    • Intro (Home): HOLA in the gradient; a picture splits it open (my headshot, then a quick flick through every project) with a ripple through the dots, then settles into the carousel. Click to skip.
    • Page transition: the cover expands full-screen, then settles into the project hero (Ross Mason reference).
    • Cursor: a simple orange circle that grows over anything clickable.
    • Headline (Home): hover "Brand designer" or "impossible to ignore": the rest dims, "Brand designer" stays dark with a "That's me!" pill above it, and "impossible to ignore" fills with the blue to orange gradient.
    • Gallery: Estrella-style meta line + serif title; hover swaps to a second image.
    • Footer (all pages but Home): dark card with its own dot field; email, LinkedIn, what I do, selected work, location and local time.
    • Project page: Josephine-style sticky left column, images on the right, previous and next project at the end.
